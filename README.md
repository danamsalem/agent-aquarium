# Agent Aquarium

**Your agents keep working. Don't tap the glass.**

Agent Aquarium is a lock screen for your Mac that shows what your AI coding agents are doing while you're away. Your Mac stays locked, your agents keep going, and everyone walking past can see that you are in fact working rather than assuming you took the 7th coffee break today.

**Website:** [agentaquarium.danamsalem.com](https://agentaquarium.danamsalem.com)

## What it does

- **Stays locked.** It runs as your screensaver, so macOS still asks for your password.
- **Live agent status.** See which sessions are working, done or waiting for you. The current session's task list is a row of cards that slides along as the agent works.
- **Leave a message.** "Back in 10." "In a meeting." Pick a preset or write your own, in any language, right-to-left included.
- **Your calendar.** Shows the meeting you're in and what's next, from Google, Apple or Outlook.
- **Needs-you alerts.** The screen glows when an agent is waiting for your approval.
- **Plan usage.** See how much of your Claude plan's 5-hour and weekly limits you've used (updated when you use `claude` in a terminal).
- **Four themes.** Warm, Retro pixel, Cosmic, and Aquarium, where every session is a little robot fish.
- **Always-on display.** Show the scene full screen on a second monitor all the time.
- **Battery estimate.** See what the lock screen costs your battery, per theme.
- **Local and private.** Everything runs on your Mac. No account, no tracking.
