# Agent Fishtank

**Your agents keep working. Don't tap the glass.**

Agent Fishtank is a lock screen for your Mac that shows what your AI coding agents are doing while you're away. Your Mac stays locked, your agents keep going, and everyone walking past can see that you are in fact working rather than assuming you took the 7th coffee break today.

**Website:** [agentfishtank.danamsalem.com](https://agentfishtank.danamsalem.com)

## What it does

- **Stays locked.** It runs as your screensaver, so macOS still asks for your password.
- **Live agent status.** See which sessions are working, done or waiting for you, with each one's current step and task list.
- **Leave a message.** "Back in 10." "In a meeting." Pick a preset or write your own.
- **Your calendar.** Shows the meeting you're in and what's next, from Google, Apple or Outlook.
- **Needs-you alerts.** The screen glows when an agent is waiting for your approval.
- **Local and private.** Everything runs on your Mac. No account, no tracking.
