Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

The short version
  • Agent Aquarium runs entirely on your Mac. We don't run servers, and we never receive your data.
  • Google Calendar access is read-only and used only to show your current and next meeting on your lock screen.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.
  • This website counts visits with GoatCounter, without cookies and without storing your IP address.

What Agent Aquarium is

Agent Aquarium ("the app", "we") is a macOS screensaver and settings app that shows the status of AI coding agents, such as Claude Code, while your Mac is locked. It also shows a custom message you choose and, if you connect a calendar, your current and next meeting.

Information the app uses

All of the following is stored only on your Mac, in your user folder, and is used only to draw your lock screen:

  • Agent activity. Status updates from your coding agents' hooks: session state (working, waiting, done), a short description of the current step, the project folder name and the agent's task list. Each session's record is deleted when the session ends, or after 24 hours.
  • Your settings. Your theme, your custom message, your saved message presets and your display options.
  • Claude plan usage. If you turn on plan usage, the app sets a status line command in Claude Code's settings that reads the usage figures Claude Code already shows there: how much of your 5-hour and weekly limits you've used, and when they reset. It saves only those figures, then runs your own status line unchanged. Turning plan usage off, or uninstalling, puts your previous status line back.
  • Battery measurements. While your Mac runs on battery, the app reads its power draw from macOS every few seconds and keeps up to 14 days of these readings, labeled with whether the lock screen was showing and in which theme, to estimate what the lock screen costs your battery.
  • Calendar links. Any iCal (.ics) address you add for Apple, Outlook or other calendars. These links are stored in a settings file that only your macOS user account can read, and are never shown on the lock screen.
  • Google sign-in. If you sign in with Google, the access and refresh tokens Google issues, your Google account's email address, and the names of your calendars with which ones you chose to show are stored in a file that only your macOS user account can read.

Google user data

If you choose Sign in with Google, the app asks for two read-only permissions: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly (see the list of calendars you're subscribed to) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly (view events on your calendars). Versions before 0.10.2 asked for the broader https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly instead; signing in again switches to the two narrower permissions. With them, the app reads:

  • your list of calendars, so you can choose which ones to show, and the address of your primary calendar, so the app can show which account is connected;
  • events from the calendars you select, for a short window from one day ago to three days ahead: the title, start and end time, status, whether the event is marked busy or free, the event type, and your own response (so declined meetings are hidden).

How it's used: only to show "In a meeting until…" and your next meeting on your own lock screen and in the settings app. Event details are kept in memory and refreshed every few minutes; they aren't written to disk.

Sharing: Google user data never leaves your Mac. It is not sent to us or to any third party, not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models. The app never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.

Limited Use: Agent Aquarium's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access: click Sign out in the app's Calendar settings. This deletes the stored tokens from your Mac and revokes them with Google. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Network connections

The app doesn't send anything to us. It connects only to:

  • Google's sign-in and Calendar services, if you sign in with Google;
  • the calendar addresses you add yourself;
  • the Agent Aquarium website (hosted on GitHub Pages), about once a day, to check whether a newer version is out. It's a single request for a small file; nothing about you or your Mac is sent with it, beyond what any web request carries, such as your IP address;
  • Google Fonts, to load the typefaces some themes and the Sign in with Google button use. Like any website request, this shares your IP address with Google; see Google Fonts privacy.

The status display runs on a local server that only accepts connections from your own Mac.

This website

The Agent Aquarium website counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It uses no cookies and doesn't follow you to other websites. For each visit it records the page, the site that linked to it, your browser and operating system, your screen size, your language and your country or region, and whether you clicked a download link. Your IP address is used only to find your location and to avoid counting the same visit twice; it isn't stored. We use these counts only to see how many people visit and where they come from. See GoatCounter's privacy policy. The app itself doesn't use GoatCounter or any other analytics.

The film page plays the film from YouTube, in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Loading that page connects to YouTube, and YouTube may store information on your device once you play the film; see Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Sign out of Google and remove calendar links in the app's settings. To uninstall, choose Uninstall Agent Aquarium… in the app's menu: it removes the Claude Code hooks, the background service and the screensaver. If you also tick Also delete my settings, presets and Google sign-in, it revokes the app's Google access and deletes everything the app stored on your Mac.

Children

The app is a developer tool and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how Google user data is used, we'll describe it in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
