Agent Aquarium

Your agents keep working. Don't tap the glass.

Agent Aquarium is a lock screen for your Mac that shows what your AI coding agents are doing while you're away. Your Mac stays locked, your agents keep going, and everyone walking past can see the work happening.

  • Stays lockedRuns as your screensaver. Your Mac still asks for your password.
  • Live agent statusEvery Claude Code session: working, done or waiting, with its task list.
  • Needs-you alertsThe screen glows when an agent is waiting for your approval.
  • Away message“Back in 10.” Pick a preset or type your own, in any language.
  • Your calendarThe meeting you're in and what's next, from Google, Apple or Outlook.
  • Plan usageHow much of your Claude plan's 5-hour and weekly limits you've used.
  • Four themesAquarium, Warm, Retro pixel and Cosmic.
  • Menu bar fishYour agents at a glance, with quick actions.
  • Keeps your Mac awakeOptional, so long runs aren't cut off by sleep.
  • Saves batteryA lighter animation when you're on battery.
  • Always-on displayKeep the scene on a second monitor.
  • Local and privateEverything runs on your Mac. No account, no tracking. Privacy policy
