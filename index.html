Agent Aquarium

Your agents keep working. Don't tap the glass.

Agent Aquarium is a lock screen for your Mac that shows what your AI coding agents are doing while you're away. Your Mac stays locked, your agents keep going, and everyone walking past can see the work happening.

Like it? Star it on GitHub and with a friend who codes with agents.

Walk away. The work doesn't stop.

Stays locked

It runs as your screensaver, so your Mac asks for your password as usual. No more leaving it unlocked because an agent is mid-task.

Live agent status

See which sessions are working, which are done, and which need you. The task list is a row of cards that slides along as the agent works.

Leave a message

"Back in 10." "In a meeting." "Do not touch." Pick a preset or type your own, in any language, and a little crab plays with the letters.

Your calendar

Shows the meeting you're in and what's next from Google, Apple or Outlook calendars, so people know when you'll be back.

Needs-you alerts

When an agent is waiting for your approval, the screen glows so you notice from across the room.

Local and private

Everything runs on your Mac. No account, no tracking, no servers of ours. It even tells you what it costs your battery. Read the privacy policy.

Plan usage

See how much of your Claude plan's 5-hour and weekly limits you've used, right under your calendar.

Four themes

Aquarium, where every session is a little robot fish swimming in the tank, plus Warm, Retro pixel, and Cosmic, with an astronaut crab on a jetpack.

Always-on display

Put the scene on a second monitor full time, so your agents' progress is always in view.
