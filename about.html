About Agent Aquarium

A lock screen for your Mac that shows what your AI coding agents are doing while you're away, so you can step away without leaving your Mac unlocked or wondering whether the work stopped.

What it is

Agent Aquarium runs as your screensaver. While your Mac is locked, it shows each Claude Code session's progress, which one needs you, your away message, your next meeting and your Claude plan usage, in one of four themes. A fish in your menu bar shows the same at a glance.

What it promises

  • Your Mac stays locked. It's a regular macOS screensaver: macOS still owns the lock and still asks for your password.
  • Your work stays on your Mac. No account, no tracking, no servers of ours. Calendar access is read-only.
  • It's free. Download it from GitHub.

Who makes it

Agent Aquarium is made by Dan Amsalem (@dannamsalem on X, danamsalem on GitHub) as a personal side project. It isn't affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. Ideas and bug reports shape what comes next: see the roadmap and give a 👍 to what you want most.
