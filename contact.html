Contact

Agent Aquarium is made by one person, so the quickest way to reach me is on GitHub.

Bugs, questions and ideas

Open an issue. There's a form for bug reports and one for ideas. For a bug, include your Agent Aquarium and macOS versions, and a screenshot if you can.

Before suggesting a feature, check the roadmap: if it's there, a 👍 on its issue is the best vote.

Security reports

Please don't open a public issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting instead: only I can see the report.

Privacy questions

What the app stores, and where, is in the privacy policy. For anything it doesn't answer, open an issue.
